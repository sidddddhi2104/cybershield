Cybersecurity Awareness

Learn essential cybersecurity practices to stay protected from phishing attacks, scams, fake websites, and social engineering threats.

Phishing Emails

Never click unknown links from suspicious emails. Always verify sender identity before opening attachments or entering credentials.

Password Safety

Use strong passwords with symbols, uppercase letters, numbers, and enable multi-factor authentication.

Fake Websites

Check HTTPS protocol, verify domains carefully, and avoid suspicious or misspelled URLs.

Online Scam Awareness

Beware of fake banking alerts, lottery scams, urgent payment requests, and social engineering attacks.
